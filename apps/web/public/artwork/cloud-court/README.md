These three painting assets and their source-coordinate puppet silhouettes are
reused from `3d-trad-chinese-art/2d` (The Cloud Court). The source paintings are
unchanged. Desktop embeds the original full Cloud Court scene, its painted
clouds, and guardian interactions through scene.html, scene.css, and scene.js.
Below 768px the app uses a swipe carousel. Both presentations clip and gently
deform the paintings using the original 76-strip puppet technique.
The Celestial, Jade, and Flame names are artistic
interpretations, not historical identifications. Source artwork remains subject
to its owner's rights.
